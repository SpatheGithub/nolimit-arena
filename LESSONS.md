# Lessons

One line per lesson, appended after each event's debrief. Each line gives the date, the event, the lesson and its evidence. When new evidence strengthens or contradicts an earlier line, or a hypothesis in `docs/FIELD_STUDY.md` (H1–H10), the new line says so.

- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Re-steal shoves over a single open at 8–30 bb were the biggest edge. Price them from the opener's fold-to-3-bet (prior ≈ 0.75, weight 4, per-seat counts). The two bots that 3-bet 4.0–4.7 times per 100 hands finished 1st and 2nd (b06 1st in 97% of resamples). Re-raise rate against steals tracked final rank (ρ ≈ 0.83). Confirms H5 (`events/full-opus-001/report/DEBRIEF.md` §1, §7).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Shove volume mattered, not shove selection. Every bot made about +1.1 bb per preflop shove in T6, whether it shoved 3.4 or 8.5 times per 100 hands. First-in shove-or-fold up to 20 bb gained +1.7 and +2.6 locally; extending it to 25 bb scored 31.6 against 34.0 in the real field, and was reverted (`bots/b04/NOTES.T6.md:61-62,94-100`).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Contradicts H3: AI-built bots over-apply ICM to calls rather than ignore it. The field folded 83% to shoves and 80% to 3-bets. The most survival-minded bots (b07, b02) had the fewest early exits but won only 10–13% of tables (DEBRIEF §2, §6 N1).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Under 100/60/40/25/15/10/5/0, early busts are cheap: 8th instead of 5th costs 15 points, 2nd instead of 1st costs 40. b04 went out 7th or 8th on 32% of tables and still finished 2nd, so optimise P(1st), not survival (`events/full-opus-001/report/REPORT.md`, "How each bot played").
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Defend the blinds by re-shoving, not by flat-calling. Per steal faced, b04 lost 0.05 bb (re-raises 20%); b07 lost 0.59 bb (calls 18%, re-raises 3.5%) (`events/full-opus-001/results/T6/profile.json`, `vs_steal`; DEBRIEF §6 N3).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Put the money mechanisms in the build; static exploits held all event.
  - **Build:** both winners' key mechanisms were already in their T1 bots (accepted at build minutes 33 and 57); patches moved real-field scores by about 1–2 points.
  - **No adaptation:** the field's fold-to-3-bet went from 78.2% (T1) to 81.2% (T6), so day-one exploits kept paying (DEBRIEF §4, §5).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Local proxies mislead.
  - **Gains that vanished in the real field:** b07's bigger ICM premium, b04's 25 bb push range, b05's looser defense.
  - **A noise drop blamed on a patch:** b08 reverted a re-steal after a "3σ" T5 drop, but the same drop recurred in T6 on the old code.
  - **Rule:** require ≥ 0 in two field types, and compare the same code across tournaments, since identical code moved 2.7 points (DEBRIEF §2).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · Heads-up conversion (42–59%) tracks time spent short-stacked (ρ ≈ 0.88): in T6 the 58–59% converters spent 2.9–3.9% of hands under 10 bb, the 42% converters 9.5–11.9%. Heads-up-only patches (b01 T4–T6, b05 T6) didn't move it. Build the chips at 8–30 bb instead (DEBRIEF §6 N5).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-001 · H1, H9 and H10 were not confirmed: 0 timeouts, errors or illegal actions in about 4.6M decisions, and 0 of 6,000 tables reached the 200-hand cap under doubling blinds. - 2026-11-01 · full-opus-002 · Strengthens the full-opus-001 re-steal lesson; this time the price came from a range model rather than a fold-to-3-bet count. b06 re-shoved over single opens at ≤ 30 bb with any hand whose EV beat 0.1 pot, assuming the opener calls with 45% of its open range. In T6 it made 7.1 3-bets per 100 hands (others 1.3–3.4), 90% all-in and 79% uncontested, for +11.4 bb/100, and gained +3.0 ± 1.8 points per table over its capped T1–T3 version (`events/full-opus-002/bots/b06/T6/agent.py:459-474`; DEBRIEF §1).
- 2026-11-01 · full-opus-002 · Gate the wide re-steal on a tight table rather than capping it by hand strength. b06 uses it only while the opener and everyone behind show VPIP ≤ 0.30 (prior 0.25, weight 8) and the opener's calls of its shoves run ≤ 1.25× the model. Ungated it scored −5.2 against the loose house zoo and +3.3/+6.0 against TAG copies; gated it scored −1.4 to +1.4 against the zoo (`events/full-opus-002/bots/b06/NOTES.T6.md:65-71,95-96`).

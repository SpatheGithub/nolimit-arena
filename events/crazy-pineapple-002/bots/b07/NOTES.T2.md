## Strategy

**Summary:** Tight-aggressive equity bot. Preflop uses a precomputed Monte Carlo equity table for every 3-card starting hand (vs 1 and vs 3 random 3-card opponents); postflop runs a time-boxed (~0.30 ms) Monte Carlo against an opponent who was dealt 3 cards and kept his best 2 on the flop, then compares range-adjusted equity with pot odds. Short stacks switch to push/fold; calls of big bets need extra equity when several players are alive (ICM).

**Before the flop:** Hands ranked by percentile (avg of HU and 4-way equity rank). Opens unopened pots to 2.2bb (+1bb per limper; 2.1bb when <25bb deep, 3bb from SB heads-up vs BB) with top 12% (early) to 27% (cutoff) / 38% (button) / 50% (SB) / 72% heads-up, wider vs tight blinds; over limpers raises top ~10% (35% as HU BB). Facing a raise: 3-bets top ~3.5% (wider vs loose raisers per observed PFR). Calls need equity vs the raiser's estimated range x realization (0.72 OOP blinds .. 0.95 HU SB) above pot odds; with 4+ players alive the hand must also be inside a fixed top-10% (+6% BB, wider vs loose raisers) calling range. At <=25bb facing a single open: re-steal shove when fold equity + equity vs the opener's calling range beats folding. When a call commits >40% of the stack or eff stack <=15bb: shove/call if equity vs the raiser's range (from PFR, preflop shove frequency, stack) beats pot odds + ICM margin.

**After the flop:** Equity vs one opponent from MC, raised to the power of the number of opponents. Bets ~75-90% pot for value when multiway equity >= 0.68 (0.62 and +15% size vs sticky opponents with low fold-to-bet); c-bets 50% pot heads-up as preflop raiser (always with eq>=0.45, else with probability ~1.1 x opponent's fold-to-bet); occasional turn/river bluff in position scaled by fold-to-bet. Facing a bet, equity is transformed to "vs betting range" ((eq-f)/(1-f), f = 0.28 + 0.22 x bet/pot + 0.15 per extra raise, +0.05 river, slightly lower vs very aggressive seats) and called if above pot odds (+4% ICM margin for big calls); raises with very strong hands. Discard: picks the 2 cards with the best MC equity (~0.40 ms).

**Short stacks and the points table:** <=13bb effective and unopened: shove when the chip EV of shoving beats folding: fold equity from each live opponent's estimated calling range (1.6/risk_bb scaled by his VPIP), equity vs that range when called, and an ICM penalty on the losing branch (8-16% of the risk with 3+ players alive). ICM margin of +3-7% equity on big calls when 3+ players remain.

**Opponents:** Per-seat VPIP, PFR, preflop shove frequency, postflop aggression (raises vs calls/checks) and fold-to-bet from hand histories. PFR/shove frequency set the raiser's range width; aggression lowers/raises the "betting range" strength assumed when facing bets; fold-to-bet scales c-bet/bluff frequency, sticky opponents get thinner and bigger value bets; VPIP scales calling ranges in the shove EV; steals more vs tight blinds.

**Last change:** v9 (009-035e0a74) for T2: smaller open-raises, 2.2bb instead of 2.5bb deep and 2.1bb instead of 2.2bb when shallow; everything else as v8 (008-5011ef61, rank 1 in T1 with 37.49 pts/table). Test: 4 copies of v9 vs 4 copies of 008 came out ahead in all three runs: +1.1 (600 tables), +0.4 (550) and +0.4 (450, using the submitted 009 package), about +0.65 pooled over 1600 tables (within noise but consistent); in a varied field of older versions (2 copies each, 400 tables) 009 33.7 vs 008 32.9. Zoo check of 009: 59.6 ± 4.3 pts/table (300 tables), max 497 us, 0 substitutions. Five other patch-window candidates were neutral or worse and were not shipped (wider steals, more c-bets, more or fewer shoves, wider 3-bets, bigger MC budget).

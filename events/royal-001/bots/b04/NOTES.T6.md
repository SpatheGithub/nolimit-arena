# Team b04 notes

## Strategy

**Summary:** Table-driven equity bot for royal hold'em (20-card deck, T-A, 4-card board). All equities are precomputed exactly (`precompute.py` -> `bot/tables.npz`): turn equity vs random / top-50% / top-25% hands for every (board, hole), flop equity averaged over turn cards, a 190x190 preflop heads-up matrix, preflop equity vs top-x% ranges and vs k random hands. Decisions are lookups plus pot-odds / chip-EV arithmetic (~10-50 us per act).

**Before the flop:** Hands ranked by equity vs one random hand (AA 69%, KK 61%, AK 60% ... TT 37%; KK is only 29% vs the top 5% because AA dominates it). Unopened with >14 BB effective: open to 2.5 BB (+1 BB per limper, +1 BB from blinds) with the top 17% (7 behind) up to 55% (SB), 70% heads-up SB; tighter with limpers. Facing a raise deep: 3-bet top 4-7%, flat the top 30% when it costs <=8% of the stack, otherwise call only when equity vs the raiser's estimated range (from observed PFR) beats pot odds by 12%. At <=14 BB: shove-or-fold by chip EV. Each player behind is assumed to call the shove with a probability learned at this table: (observed calls of preflop shoves >=5 BB + 0.22 x 4) / (shoves faced + 4), clamped 8-70%. Equity is computed vs that calling range. Facing all-ins or committing raises: call when equity vs the shover's estimated range (PFR-based, >=35% wide for short shovers, >=50% heads-up, >=30% for late-position steals) beats pot odds +2%; shove over it with equity > 0.55.

**After the flop:** Strength = exact equity from tables vs random / top-50% / top-25% hands, raised to the number of opponents. Value-bets 60-75% pot when equity vs top-50% >= 0.62 flop / 0.58 turn (0.04 lower vs players who rarely fold). Facing a bet: small bets -> blend of top-50% and random-hand equity; half pot+ -> top-50% equity; pot+ or any raise -> top-25% equity; widened vs very aggressive bettors. Calls when that beats pot odds +3%; raises when top-25% equity^n >= 0.72. Heads-up: semi-bluffs the flop with equity >= 0.5 and bluffs low-equity hands at a rate tied to the opponent's fold rate.

**Short stacks and the points table:** Payouts 100/25/5 are top-heavy, so the bot plays close to chip-EV (small +2% margin on all-in calls); push/fold below 14 BB. Heads-up and blind-vs-blind (only SB and BB left in the hand) at <=25 BB effective, it plays exact heads-up push/fold Nash ranges for royal. These were computed by fictitious play on the 190x190 heads-up equity matrix (`hu_nash.py` -> `bot/hunash.npz`). The small blind first in shoves or folds; the big blind facing a small-blind all-in (or a raise >=60% of its stack) calls or folds. Mixed frequencies use the bot's own RNG. Examples: at 10 BB the small blind shoves 60% and the big blind calls 47%; at 15 BB, 61% / 33%; at 20 BB, 53% / 26%.

**Opponents:** Per-seat counters from hand histories: VPIP, PFR, postflop aggression, calls, folds. Used for the raiser's range width, bluff frequency, value thresholds vs calling stations, and widening calls vs maniacs.

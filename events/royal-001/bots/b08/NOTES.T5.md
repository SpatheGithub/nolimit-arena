# Team b08 notes

## Strategy

**Summary:** Equity-driven bot for royal hold'em (20-card deck, 4-card board). All hand strength comes from tables precomputed for this deck (preflop equity vs 1..7 random hands, exact rank-only head-to-head equities for all 15x15 starting-hand types, flop/turn equity vs random); at decision time it computes equity against an estimated opponent range (top-X% of hands by strength, card-removal aware) and compares it with pot odds or a shove EV model. Points 100/25/5 are winner-take-most, so it plays close to chip-EV and leans aggressive.

**Before the flop:** Opens by strength percentile (HU equity order: AA > KK > AK > AQ > QQ ~ AJ > KQ > AT > KJ > JQ ~ KT ~ JJ > QT > TJ > TT). The threshold depends on how many players are left to act: about the top 30% UTG, 58% on the button and 65% in the SB. It opens about 88% heads-up and a little wider 3-handed, and loosens against seats that often fold to raises. Opens to 2.5bb (2.2bb under 30bb). Facing a raise, it estimates the raiser's range from observed PFR (default top 30%; top 14% for a 3-bet). It 3-bets or shoves premiums and calls when range equity beats pot odds plus a realization penalty: 8 points when deep, 4 under 25bb (doubled in v6, so fewer flat calls; VPIP about 38%). At 22bb or less it shoves light against raisers who fold a lot.

**After the flop:** Enumerates every opponent rank pair (and, on the flop, every turn card) to get equity against each opponent's range. The range is the top fraction of hands by board strength. Bets narrow it: bigger bets make it tighter, adjusted for that seat's observed aggression. Bets about 70% pot with equity >= 0.58 against those ranges (0.55 heads-up; 0.62/0.58 before v6). The threshold moves 0.04 lower against calling stations and 0.02 higher against folders. Heads-up c-bet frequency is 0.55, adjusted by the opponent's observed fold-to-bet rate (20–85%). It bluffs the turn against high-fold seats. It calls when equity beats pot odds and raises with equity >= 0.80.

**Short stacks and the points table:** At 16bb effective or less it plays push/fold when first in. It shoves when an EV model beats folding: each player behind calls with their top c% of hands, where c comes from that player's VPIP, is larger in the BB and smaller against big shoves. Facing a single non-all-in raise when short, it uses the normal rule: call when range equity beats pot odds plus the penalty, and commit or fold when the call is 40% or more of its stack. The re-shove rule from v7 and v8 is off. It calls shoves when equity against the shover's estimated range beats pot odds. That range blends a stack-depth default with the seat's observed shove rate. When stacks are deep (over 30bb, 3+ players alive), a shove or huge raise is read as a tight range (about the top 12%, wider for high-PFR seats), and stacking off needs a 4-point equity margin over pot odds. The same applies postflop: a bet of 45% or more of our deep stack shrinks the bettor's range by 25%. There is no ICM tightening, because 1st place is worth 4x 2nd.

**Opponents:** Tracks per seat: hands, VPIP, PFR, fold-to-raise preflop, preflop shove rate, postflop aggression (raises vs calls) and postflop fold-to-bet rate. These drive steal thresholds, calling-range estimates for shoves, raiser/shover/bettor range widths, and bluff and thin-value frequencies.

**Last change:** v9 (009-73a10724) turns the short-stack re-shove rule off (sf_short=0), so it behaves exactly like v6: v5 plus fewer flat calls (call_pen 0.08) and thinner value bets (bet_eq 0.58/0.55). Real results by version: v5 21.52 (T2, rank 1), v7 (with re-shove) 20.48 (T3, rank 1), v8 (EV re-shove) 17.77 (T4, rank 3). 1st places fell 182 to 165 to 143, and HU conversion 62% to 54% to 56%. Local tests never showed the re-shove helping (v8 vs v6 mean 0; v7 vs v6 +0.7), and v8 overestimated an opener's fold rate. v6's parameters beat v5 by 2-3 points in five local runs (latest 4v4, 400 tables: v9 17.5 vs v5 15.0). In a house-bot field v9 was level with v5 and v8, with 0 substitutions. Full version history is in the log below.
